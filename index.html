Build options

Site-built or modular, engineered for your climate.

We deliver each plant either built on site or as a modular building, whichever suits the location, timeline and budget. Both use certified components suitable for various climates.

Modular plants are assembled in Bahrain or Oman and delivered by road, and scale from a single 10 tonnes per day line to a multi-plant cluster.

The table below summarises the build options.

Build methodSite-built or modular
ComponentsCertified insulated panels and plant components suitable for various climates
Climate rangeSpecified for hot, humid, temperate or cold sites
Modular assemblyBahrain or Oman, with delivery by road
Typical plant sizes10 and 20 tonnes per day, single or double plants
